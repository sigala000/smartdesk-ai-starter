# Billing Responsibility

Meta bills each client company directly for WhatsApp usage. The client configures
its payment method in WhatsApp Manager. SmartDesk stores only a coarse projection
(`unknown`, `ready`, `action_required`, or `not_applicable`) and never stores card
numbers, payment credentials, invoices, or a shared Meta credit line.

SmartDesk billing is separate. The database models trials, a provider-neutral
plan identifier, seat and usage limits, grace, suspension, and cancellation. No
payment provider, price, tax, currency, or commercial term is selected in Phase
10. Pilot activation is manual and audited until the owner approves a provider.
