# Client Onboarding Runbook

1. The company owner registers, verifies email, signs in, and creates the company.
2. The owner completes the profile, adds at least one department and service,
   reviews knowledge, and activates the workspace.
3. The owner invites employees. Invitations expire after seven days and can be
   revoked. Keep at least one active administrator.
4. Under **WhatsApp**, the owner acknowledges that Meta billing is separate and
   selects **Connect WhatsApp with Meta**.
5. The owner authenticates only in Meta's window, selects company-owned assets,
   and completes any OTP, PIN, business verification, or billing step directly
   with Meta. SmartDesk support must never request these values.
6. The connection remains `test_pending`. Send a non-sensitive,
   customer-initiated test message. Confirm the SmartDesk reply and dashboard
   tenant. Only then is the connection `active`.
7. Record dated evidence of the end-to-end request confirmation and exact-one
   ticket result. Stop if the destination maps incorrectly or Meta reports a
   permission, quality, policy, billing, or number-ownership error.
